# 9 Classical Authors

English-language corpus of 9 authors, 2 books each, one text file per chapter, UTF-8.

## Files

`<author>/<book>/<chapter number>.txt`:

- `bronteAnne/`: *Agnes Grey*, *The Tenant of Wildfell Hall*
- `bronteCharlote/`: *Jane Eyre*, *The Professor*
- `Cooper/`: *The Last of the Mohicans*, *The Spy*
- `Emerson/`: *The Conduct of Life*, *English Traits*
- `Hawthorne/`: *Doctor Grimshawe's Secret*, *The House of the Seven Gables*
- `Melwille/`: *Moby-Dick*, *Redburn*
- `Shaw/`: *Misalliance*, *Pygmalion*
- `Thoreau/`: *A Week on the Concord and Merrimack Rivers*, *Walden*
- `wilde/`: *An Ideal Husband*, *A Woman of No Importance*

(Directory names are kept as they are, typos included, since code depends on them.)

## Source

The books' texts from Project Gutenberg (https://www.gutenberg.org), split into chapters
and stripped of the Project Gutenberg header and license for the course.
