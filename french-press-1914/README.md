# French Press 1914

OCR'd full text of every 1914 issue of two French daily newspapers: *L'Humanité* (360
issues) and *Le Figaro* (24 issues, between January 19 and November 18). A small extract of the
BnF's *Europeana Newspapers French corpus*.

## Files

- `<newspaper>/1914/<YYYYMMDD>/<YYYYMMDD>.metadata.fulltext.json`: one issue, as a JSON
  object with its metadata (`title`, `date`, `publisher`, `source`, `rights`, ...) and
  its OCR'd text (`contentAsText`). The OCR is raw and has many errors.

## Source

BnF (Bibliothèque nationale de France), *Europeana Newspapers French corpus (text
format)*, from the newspapers digitized in Gallica (https://gallica.bnf.fr) during the
Europeana Newspapers project: http://api.bnf.fr/europeana-newspapers-french-corpus-text-format
