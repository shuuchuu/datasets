# French départements

Reference tables of the French départements and their regions, to join with other
datasets (e.g. `../dvf`).

## Files

- `departements-region.csv`: columns `num_dep`, `dep_name`, `region_name`.
  - Source: *Départements et leurs régions*, data.gouv.fr,
    https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/departements-et-leurs-regions (resource
    `987227fb-dcb2-429e-96af-8979f97c9c84`, 2019-08-15; its publisher's account has since
    been deleted).
  - License: ODC PDDL (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/), public domain.
- `departements-france.csv`: columns `code_departement`, `nom_departement`,
  `code_region`, `nom_region`.
  - Source: *Departements de france*, data.gouv.fr,
    https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/departements-de-france (resource
    `70cef74f-70b1-495a-8500-c089229c0254`, 2020-04-25).
  - License: ODC ODbL (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/): attribute the
    source, and share a database derived from it under the ODbL.
