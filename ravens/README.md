# Ravens of the Tower of London

A colour photograph of Jubilee and Munin, two of the ravens of the Tower of London,
perched on a railing in front of the White Tower: the image the image-processing
chapters of shuuchuu's Python course load, filter and transform (Pillow, OpenCV,
scikit-image).

## Files

- `ravens.jpg`: the photograph, cropped square and scaled down to 1024 × 1024 pixels
  (JPEG, RGB).

## Source

Colin (Wikimedia Commons user Colin), 30 April 2016, via Wikimedia Commons,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jubilee_and_Munin,_Ravens,_Tower_of_London_2016-04-30.jpg
(cropped and scaled down by Shuuchuu).
