# Rosie the Riveter (Vultee), 1943

A colour photograph of a real-life "Rosie the Riveter" operating a hand drill at
Vultee-Nashville, Tennessee, on an A-31 Vengeance dive bomber: her red bandana, mirrored
in the metal sheet, stands out against muted surroundings, which suits colour isolation
exercises (the Pillow and NumPy lab keeps the red of the bandana and turns the rest
grey).

## Files

- `rosie-the-riveter.jpg`: the photograph, scaled down to 1280 × 1023 pixels (JPEG).
